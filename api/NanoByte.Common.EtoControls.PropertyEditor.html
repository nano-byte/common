Remarks

Respects BrowsableAttribute, CategoryAttribute, DisplayNameAttribute, DescriptionAttribute, DefaultValueAttribute, PasswordPropertyTextAttribute and RangeAttribute. Uses check boxes for bools, drop-downs for enums, numeric steppers for ints and text boxes with TypeConverters for everything else.
